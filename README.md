# 🏪 Vyapari Mitra

### AI-Powered Smart Business Management System for Vendors

**TechFest 5.0 – CODE-A-THONE**

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Vyapari Mitra is a smart digital platform built to empower small and medium vendors by simplifying their daily business operations using modern technology and Artificial Intelligence.

It centralizes **sales tracking, expense management, employee handling, attendance tracking, salary processing, and order management** into a single unified dashboard, enabling vendors to make smarter decisions, reduce manual effort, and improve overall business efficiency.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🚀 Features

Our platform is designed with modular functionality to cover every major aspect of vendor operations.

### 📊 Dashboard

Provides a quick overview of business performance.

* Sales overview
* Expense summary
* Business insights at a glance

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 📦 Order Management

Helps vendors manage the complete order lifecycle digitally.

* Manage Pending, Completed, and Cancelled orders
* Add new orders
* Update order status in real time

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 🛒 Product Management

Allows vendors to organize their shop inventory efficiently.

* Add and manage product list
* Maintain inventory records
* Update or delete products

----------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 💸 Expense Management

Tracks all business-related expenses to calculate real profit.

* Track selling, stock, and maintenance expenses
* Categorize expenses
* View total expense summary

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 👥 Employee Management

Simplifies staff handling with centralized employee records.

* Add and manage employee details
* Store role, contact information, and salary records
* Edit or remove employee data

--------------------------------------------------------------

### 📅 Attendance Management

Digitizes daily attendance tracking.

* Mark employees as Present or Absent
* Maintain daily attendance records
* Attendance linked with salary calculation

-------------------------------------------------

### 💰 Salary Management

Automates salary calculation based on attendance and deductions.

* Calculate salary automatically
* Display final payable amount
* Process salary directly from dashboard

-------------------------------------------------------

### 🤖 AI Sales Prediction (If Implemented)

Uses historical data to assist in future planning.

* Predict weekly sales
* Help vendors plan inventory
* Reduce overstock and losses

-----------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Small and medium vendors often rely on manual record keeping, which leads to:

* Inaccurate profit calculation
* Poor inventory planning
* Manual attendance and salary handling
* Order mismanagement

These challenges result in inefficiency, financial loss, and lack of data-driven decisions.

Vyapari Mitra addresses these issues by providing a **digital, AI-powered management system** that replaces manual processes with smart automation.

------------------------------------------------------------

##System Architecture (Conceptual)

Vendor → Dashboard → Database → AI Model → Reports & Insights

This flow ensures real-time data storage, intelligent analysis, and actionable insights for vendors.
